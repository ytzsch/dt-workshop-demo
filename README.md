# D&T Workshop Manager – demo

A clickable demo of a workshop manager for school Design and Technology departments: pupils send laser and 3D print jobs, the teacher approves them, and the technician nests them on sheets from stock and tracks materials and safety inductions.

Everything runs in the browser. The school (Hillside Academy) and pupils are made up, nothing is saved, and reloading the page resets the demo.

Live page: https://ytzsch.github.io/dt-workshop-demo/
